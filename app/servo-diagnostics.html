Read the hardware, without changing it

Close the factory software and disconnect any other NexArm browser tab first. Leave the arm stationary in its supported, clear setup. Do not restrain a powered joint or create an obstruction for this scan.

This page sends only firmware and register-read requests. It has no movement, Home, torque, calibration, or setting-write controls. Opening USB may affect controller startup; this diagnostic does not establish protection against pinching.

Disconnected. Nothing is requested until you connect and choose Read diagnostics.

One finite scan per connection: factory configuration reads first, then short direct-position probes. Broader register reads and three status samples are attempted only for joints whose short probe replies. Up to 49 requests, one at a time. Missing replies are not zero readings. Stop reads cancels future requests; it is not an emergency stop for the arm. Reconnect to repeat a scan.

Results stay in memory until downloaded. The export includes a bounded raw serial capture, which may contain controller debug text; review it before sharing. No accounts, uploads, browser storage, or robot settings are involved. The browser does not request USB device identifiers.
