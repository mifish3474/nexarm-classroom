Each click targets the current reported value −20 or +20. Wait for that move to finish before the next click. Buttons remain reusable; there is no attempt counter. Raw values are servo units.

Increasing values may raise or lower a joint, or open or close the gripper. Keep its pinch area clear. These teacher controls rely on your view of the arm; mechanical and collision limits are not calibrated.

Home is this target pose; it does not reset servo calibration. A difference of up to 9 raw units is accepted without repeated corrections. Stop cancels future steps and leaves the USB connection open; a command already sent cannot be recalled.

Manual positioning: release and restore holding

Support the arm before releasing; it can fall. Keep fingers out of joint and jaw pinch areas. Gently place it only after confirming release; never force resistance. Continue supporting it during restore and verify that it holds the placed pose.

An interrupted release leaves holding unverified across reload. Reconnect and restore or physically confirm holding before enabling movement. There is no automatic release or restore.
