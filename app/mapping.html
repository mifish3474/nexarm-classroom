Teacher: manual positioning

Release holding power, gently place the supported arm, then restore holding. These controls use the factory All Off / All On command path. They do not move to Home or change calibration. Joint tests and returns are disabled throughout manual setup.

Support the arm before release; it can fall under its own weight. Empty the gripper and clear all joint/jaw pinch areas. Never force resistance. Continue supporting the arm during restore: the teacher observed it hold the newly placed pose, but unexpected movement remains possible.

Button completion means the command was written, not that torque was measured. Stop/Disconnect and page hiding send no holding command. After interruption or reload, reconnect and restore or verify holding before any movement. If holding was restored in the factory app, you can confirm it here after reconnecting and checking fresh readings.

Factory controls as an alternative

The teacher confirmed All Off releases the arm, All On holds a manually placed pose in the tested case, factory Read Pos refreshes its displayed numbers, and our passive inspector matches those refreshed readings. This supports using the guarded manual controls above; it is not a guarantee for every pose or fault.

You can also use the factory app: export and Stop/Disconnect here first, support the arm, then use its All Off / All On controls. Keep the gripper empty, avoid pinch areas, and never force resistance. Avoid All Home and Calibration Center for this setup. Close that app before reconnecting here. If the browser shows Recovery required after factory holding was restored, inspect fresh readings and use Confirm holding restored; it sends nothing.

If readings do not follow a manually changed pose or holding behaves unexpectedly, stop before a return/test and report the observation. Manual positioning does not redefine Reference A or bypass the commissioning region.
